A lawyer is paid separately, at ₦9,000 for 10 minutes. If you're in danger or someone is detained right now, Juris gives you the safety steps whether you have credit or not.

Which languages can I use?

English, Pidgin, Hausa, Yorùbá and Igbo. Choose one when you start, or just write or speak in your language and Juris follows. Type LANGUAGE any time to switch.

Can Juris go to court or the police station for me?

No. Juris gives information and next steps. If you need someone to write, call or appear for you, a verified lawyer can take that on.

Is my conversation private?

Yes. Your chat stays between you and Juris. A lawyer sees your case only after you ask for one.

Which states do you cover?

All 36 states and the FCT. Juris asks your state because some laws, like tenancy laws, differ from state to state.
