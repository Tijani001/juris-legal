Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Juris is an AI legal information assistant available on WhatsApp. It is operated by Ostria Technology Development Limited ("we", "us"), based in Nigeria. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use Juris, why, and what rights you have. It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to anyone who messages Juris on WhatsApp, including clients and our operators.

2. Data we collect

  • WhatsApp identifiers: your phone number and the display name WhatsApp provides.
  • Messages: the text you send, and voice notes you send, which we transcribe into text.
  • Our replies: the answers Juris sends you, stored so we can continue the conversation and keep records.
  • Usage and account data: your language choice, free replies used, credit balance, subscription plan and expiry, and voice-reply setting.
  • Payment records: when you pay, our payment provider (Paystack) processes your card or bank details. We receive a payment reference and amount. We do not store card numbers.
  • Consent record: the time you agreed to these terms.

3. How we use your data

  • To answer your legal questions and continue your conversation.
  • To transcribe voice notes so Juris can understand them.
  • To manage free replies, credits, subscriptions and payments.
  • To connect you with a human lawyer when you ask for one and pay for that service.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix problems.

4. Legal basis

We rely on your consent (when you reply to agree to these terms) and on the contract between you and us when you use paid features. You can withdraw consent at any time by sending STOP, though we may need to keep some records where the law requires it.

5. Who we share data with

We use service providers to run Juris. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Meta (WhatsApp): delivers messages between you and Juris.
  • OpenAI: generates Juris's answers from your messages.
  • Voice transcription providers: YarnGPT, Groq (Whisper), and where enabled, N-ATLAS, which turn voice notes into text.
  • Paystack: processes payments.
  • n8n: hosts the automation that connects WhatsApp to Juris.
  • A lawyer you choose to be connected with: we share your name and phone number and the issue you describe, only when you ask for a lawyer and pay for that service.

Some of these providers are outside Nigeria, including in the United States and the European Union. Where we transfer data abroad, we do so under safeguards the Act permits.

6. How long we keep data

We keep conversation records for 12 months after your last activity. We keep payment records for six years, to meet tax and accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete your data earlier, as described below.

7. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold your data and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or object to some processing;
  • withdraw consent.

To exercise these rights, email ibntijani@gmail.com from the number you use on WhatsApp. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including access controls and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you if a breach affects you as the law requires.

9. Children

Juris is for people aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has used Juris, contact us and we will delete the data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page and change the date above.
